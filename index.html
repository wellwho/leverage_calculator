Fees are ignored. There's no leverage or liquidation here — every dollar of "Available balance" is spent directly on the asset. Buy sizes grow geometrically (×1.26 per step) up to the buy nearest the "Peak buy weight" you set above (a percentage of the ladder's own depth, default 50% — e.g. 50% of a -70% ladder lands the peak at -35%), then shrink geometrically beyond it, spaced evenly across the ladder down to the requested depth. If the resulting peak lands beyond the ladder's own reach, it clamps to the last buy and sizing grows the whole way instead. Buy #1 executes as a market order (fills immediately at the live price); every other buy is a limit order resting at its ladder price. Uses the exact same trigger prices as the Leveraged tab for the same inputs, and the same buy-size shape when the peak is left at its default.

Orders since your last Execute — not the pair's full trading history. "Filled" rows show how deep this run's ladder actually went; "Resting" ones are still on the book. "Avg entry if filled" is the running position average as if every order down to that row has triggered — a cumulative preview of where your average lands at each rung, same idea as the Ladder table's Avg entry column but built from these real orders. Canceled/invalid orders don't contribute and show "—". While a position is open, "Execute plan on Bybit" is hidden below so you don't accidentally deploy a second ladder on top of it — the Calculate button above still works for previewing numbers only.
